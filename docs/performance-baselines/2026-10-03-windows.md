# Windows performance investigation — 2026-10-03

Environment: Windows 11 build 26300, Intel Family 6 Model 183, NVIDIA GeForce
RTX 4070 Ti SUPER, DX12, Python 3.12.14, release/locked dependencies.
Base commit: `b7f55e1f49243eeb397e6884b201c4648f637d36`; after is the working-tree
change accompanying this report. These are local observations, not CI thresholds.

## Findings and changes

The bare Alacritty 0.26.0 parser comparison exposed about 1.5× ASCII and 2×
ANSI/Unicode extension overhead. The graphics stream now borrows complete ordinary
text/CSI reads instead of allocating and reconstructing CSI sequences. Fragmented
sequences, graphics, OSC and cell-size queries retain the incremental path.
The per-character handler keeps image bookkeeping outside its inline ordinary
text path.

Tracing also exposed repeated shaping of identical isolated Unicode cells, with
long pane-update intervals during sustained output. The renderer now shares
immutable shaped buffers for equal text, width and effective glyph attributes
across grid positions. Selection foreground and hyperlink styling participate
in the key. Each pane retains at most 512 entries and invalidates them on layout,
color, selection and font changes. Sized text retains its existing shaping path.

## Production PTY-to-present baseline

Same harness, fixtures, actual grids (141×41), font configuration and bundled
ConPTY for both builds. Three fresh-process samples per case after a warmup;
medians below include startup-warmed but otherwise cold font/content caches.
The producer emits completion markers and the harness waits for successful
presentation. Trace instrumentation is identical. Durations include transport,
parsing and rendering; stage totals overlap and must not be added.

| Content | Size | Before ms | After ms | Duration change |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| ascii | 1 MiB | 72.6 | 70.4 | -3.0% |
| ascii | 10 MiB | 243.4 | 232.0 | -4.7% |
| ansi | 1 MiB | 63.8 | 64.8 | +1.5% |
| ansi | 10 MiB | 271.5 | 244.8 | -9.9% |
| unicode | 1 MiB | 139.8 | 66.3 | -52.6% |

Small differences (especially the ASCII and ANSI cases) need more runs to
establish significance. The strongest repeatable gain is Unicode/mixed-content
rendering. No improvement in search, Ruby or image decoding is claimed.

## Parser-only measurements

Five timing batches per workload after warmup, 100×40 grid and 2,000-line history.
These are wrapper and bare-core scores, not Alacritty GUI scores.

| Wrapper workload | Before ns/op | After ns/op | Duration change |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| ASCII | 38,685 | 30,644 | -20.8% |
| ANSI | 32,339 | 23,320 | -27.9% |
| ANSI + Unicode | 37,918 | 27,738 | -26.8% |

The final bare-core measurements were 25,000 / 16,847 / 20,685 ns/op respectively.
The remaining wrapper overhead is about 1.23× / 1.38× / 1.34× in these samples.
Snapshot/search timings were reviewed but are outside the changed paths.

## Other terminals

WezTerm `20240203-110809-5046fc22` was installed and measured with the common
DSR harness. Both applications reported 100×40, 2,000-line history, font size 14;
WezTerm used its default font and toyoterm generic monospace. These are installed
application observations, not equal-font/equal-PTY engine rankings. DSR responds
after parsing, not after final-frame presentation. Five samples after warmup,
10 MiB per output case and 1,000 alternate-screen redraws:

| Workload | WezTerm ms | toyoterm before ms | toyoterm final ms |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| ascii | 502.2 | 86.8 | 67.5 |
| ansi | 1200.0 | 136.1 | 148.7 |
| unicode | 1636.5 | 162.2 | 145.3 |
| redraw | 139.4 | 19.1 | 11.6 |

The large WezTerm gap is substantially influenced by Windows console/PTY
transport; toyoterm uses its bundled preview ConPTY. It is not attributable to
this patch. Earlier diagnostic runs relocated the toyoterm executable without
its ConPTY companions and selected OS ConPTY, producing roughly 0.5–1.6 second
results; these incompatible runs are excluded from the paired GUI table.
Sustained DSR ANSI timings did not improve, despite the parser-only gain.

Alacritty GUI, Kitty and Ghostty were unavailable locally. Their primary
benchmark methods were reviewed: [vtebench](https://github.com/alacritty/vtebench),
[Kitty](https://sw.kovidgoyal.net/kitty/performance/) and
[Ghostty](https://github.com/ghostty-org/ghostty/blob/main/src/benchmark/AGENTS.md).
Published Linux/macOS numbers cannot establish how toyoterm ranks on this
Windows machine. Equal-platform comparisons, input latency, energy use and
feature-equivalent image/split/search comparisons remain outstanding.
